# 👋 Hello There!

I'm **Pulkit Verma**, a **GenAI & Machine Learning Engineer** currently working at **KPMG**, with **4.8 years of experience** in building software and AI-powered solutions. My focus is on designing practical, production-oriented **agentic AI systems**, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** applications, and **intelligent document processing** workflows.

I work across the AI application lifecycle—from data ingestion, OCR, embeddings, retrieval, and LLM orchestration to backend APIs, evaluation, containerization, and cloud deployment. I enjoy turning complex requirements into reliable, maintainable systems using **Python**, **FastAPI**, **LangGraph**, **PostgreSQL/pgvector**, and **Google Cloud**.

I'm particularly interested in **AI agents**, **enterprise knowledge assistants**, **LLM/RAG evaluation**, and the engineering challenges of making AI systems secure, observable, and useful in real-world environments. Feel free to explore my projects or connect to discuss GenAI, ML, or engineering.

## 🎯 Core Expertise

- **Agentic AI & Workflow Orchestration** — Building tool-using AI workflows, agent collaboration, human-in-the-loop steps, guardrails, and controlled automation with LangGraph and LangChain.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — Document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, semantic retrieval, vector search, answer grounding, and enterprise knowledge assistants.
- **LLM & RAG Evaluation** — Evaluating retrieval and generation quality with DeepEval, Pytest, and metrics such as Recall@K, Precision@K, MRR, and nDCG.
- **Intelligent Document Processing** — OCR, information extraction, document verification, and computer vision pipelines with PyTorch, Transformers, OpenCV, and TrOCR.
- **Backend & Full-Stack Engineering** — Developing APIs and AI applications with FastAPI, Python, TypeScript, and modern frontend tooling.
- **Data & Model Engineering** — Machine learning, NLP, classification, predictive modeling, experiment workflows, and practical model selection.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** — Dockerized services, background task processing, database-backed applications, and deployment on Google Cloud.
